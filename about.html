MASTER CORPORATE NARRATIVE

Single-Point Turnkey Accountability in Infrastructure

VISIONTECH Engineering Ltd. (VEL) is a dynamic EPC contractor specializing in civil and electro-mechanical works across Bangladesh. The company delivers high-quality, cost-effective, and sustainable engineering solutions across infrastructure, power transmission, and industrial sectors.

Through strategic synergy with sister concern Swadesh Carrying, VisionTech unites heavy foundation civil casting, high-voltage substation switchgear setup, and heavy multi-axle port logistics under one coordinated roof.

Corporate Mission

To provide reliable, innovative, and sustainable engineering solutions in civil and electro-mechanical sectors, ensuring quality, safety, and client satisfaction in every project.

Corporate Vision

To become a leading EPC contractor in Bangladesh and beyond, recognized for excellence, innovation, and contribution to national infrastructure and energy development.

OCCUPATIONAL HEALTH & SAFETY POLICY (PAGE 25)

Zero-Harm EHS Standard & Site Governance

Strict adherence to ISO 9001 and ISO 45001 protocols, zero-accident mandates, and mandatory life-safety systems across all active worksites.

1. Zero Accident Mandate

Zero fatal incident record across 40+ substation sites and highway projects. Strict pre-task Job Safety Analysis (JSA) and daily site safety tool-box talks.

2. Work Permit System (PTW)

Mandatory signed Permit-to-Work prior to entering live high-voltage bays, confined spaces, hot welding operations, and deep trenching exceeding 1.5m.

3. 100% Certified PPE Adherence

Arc-flash certified safety helmets, 33kV dielectric safety shoes, eye shields, high-visibility vests, and double-lanyard full-body safety harnesses for elevated erection.

4. Traffic & Urban Road Safety

Continuous deployment of certified traffic marshals, perimeter barricades, reflective chevron cones, and night-vision strobe blinkers on highway and metropolitan cable corridors.

5. Lifting Machinery & Rigging Check

Daily and monthly third-party mechanical integrity inspections of piling rigs, roof hoists, crane wire ropes, hydraulic winches, and shackles before heavy lifts.

6. Housekeeping & Environmental Care

Continuous site cleanup, dust suppression watering, safe hazardous material containment, concrete washout management, and zero environmental contamination.
