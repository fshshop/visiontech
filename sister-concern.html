Multimodal Heavy Lift Transport

Low bed trailer (Low-bed)s, hydraulic multi-axle modular transporters, and heavy prime movers engineered for massive 100-300 ton transformers, turbine rotors, gas exchangers, and boilers over challenging terrain.

  • Low bed trailer (Low-bed)s & hydraulic modular axles
  • River transport barges & landing craft pontoons
  • Mobile cranes & telescopic boom cranes
All Land & Sea Ports Covered

Unloading, Dragging & Plinth Placement

Safe indigenous dragging and skidding methods using heavy winches, greased steel plates, and 400mm hardwood sleepers to position massive transformers onto civil plinths with zero foundation shock.

  • Synchronized hydraulic jacks (1.5x rating)
  • High-capacity winches & wire ropes (no chain blocks)
  • Strict ≤10 degree tilt control protocol
Zero-Impact Plinth Grounding

Oil Purification & On-Site Testing

High-vacuum degasification and oil dehydration equipment for transformers dispatched gas-filled or oil-filled. Complete with mobile testing equipment to certify BDV, moisture, and insulation resistance on site.

  • 5,000 LPH (5000 lph) multi-stage vacuum oil purifier
  • 25 kL mobile oil storage tanks with pump
  • High-precision ≥100 kV oil BDV test kits
760 mm Hg Vacuum & BDV > 70kV
