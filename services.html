DISCIPLINE MATRIX

1. EPC Turnkey Verticals

Comprehensive EPC execution across power grids, heavy industrial manufacturing, renewable energy, and transportation corridors.

1.1 ENERGY & POWER

400kV, 230kV & 132kV Turnkey GIS Substations & Transmission

VisionTech delivers apex turnkey GIS and AIS substation construction across Bangladesh with sovereign utilities including PGCB, DPDC, and BREB. Proven benchmarks include Bhulta 400/230/132kV GIS, Purbasadipur 230/132kV, Madunaghat 230kV Upgradation, Feni 230/132kV, and Maijdee 132/33kV. In underground transmission, VEL manufactured and installed 100,000+ certified concrete cable protection slabs (DPDC Signboard to Siddhirganj, BDT 18.3M).

GIS Control Buildings Multi-storied reinforced switching stations & basements
Underground Cable Slabs Interlocking high-strength precast protection slabs
1.2 INDUSTRIAL & MANUFACTURING

Heavy Factory Civil, PEB Structures & Industrial MEP Systems

Complete engineering design and site construction for industrial processing complexes, textile mills, and corporate infrastructure. Key credentials include Onin Textile Mills perimeter security & heavy MEP drainage works (BDT 12.6M), PTC PLC (Bangladesh Petroleum Corporation subsidiary) corporate headquarters renovation, and deep equipment foundation pads engineered for dynamic machinery vibration absorption.

Pre-Engineered Buildings (PEB) Long-span structural steel factory bays & warehouses
Industrial MEP Networks High-capacity busbar trunking, HVAC & fire safety loops
1.3 RENEWABLE ENERGY

Utility Solar PV Parks, Industrial Rooftops & Grid Tie-in EPC

Accelerating Bangladesh's clean energy transition through turnkey solar photovoltaic (PV) generation systems. VisionTech integrates ground-mounted solar arrays, industrial net-metered rooftop solar installations, battery energy storage systems (BESS), and specialized step-up substations connecting renewable megawatts smoothly to the national grid.

Utility & Rooftop Solar Tier-1 PV modules, string/central inverters & mounting
Grid Synchronization Harmonic filtering, metering & sovereign grid tie-in
1.4 INFRASTRUCTURE & TRANSPORTATION

Highway Bridges, Viaducts, Deep Piling & Transport Links

Execution of mission-critical civil infrastructure that drives national mobility. Our record includes auxiliary civil works on the landmark Kanchpur-Meghna-Gumti 2nd Bridges (Package PW-01), heavy foundation piling, RC box culverts, bridge approach ramps, and deep drainage civil networks built to withstand severe subtropical hydrological conditions.

Bridge Auxiliary Works Approaches, retaining walls, slope protection & spans
Heavy Civil Transport Hubs Port corridors, container yards & terminal aprons
